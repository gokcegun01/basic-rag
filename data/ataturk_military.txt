Mustafa Kemal Atatürk was born in 1881 in Salonica. His military brilliance first gained international recognition during World War I in 1915, where he successfully commanded the Ottoman forces during the Gallipoli campaign, achieving a crucial victory at Anafartalar.

He officially initiated the Turkish War of Independence on May 19, 1919, by landing in the northern port city of Samsun. To establish a national government, he led the opening of the Grand National Assembly (TBMM) in Ankara on April 23, 1920, and was elected as its first president.

Following the decisive victory at the Great Offensive (Büyük Taarruz) in August 1922, the war came to an end. The international recognition of the new Turkish state was finalized with the signing of the Treaty of Lausanne on July 24, 1923, which secured the nation's sovereign borders.
